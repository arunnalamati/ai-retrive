Employee Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least three working days before the intended leave date.

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

For sick leave lasting more than two consecutive working days, a medical certificate may be required.

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Casual leave is intended for short and unexpected personal requirements.

Employees may request work from home for up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.

All planned leave requests must be approved by the employee's reporting manager.

Public holidays are separate from annual leave and are not deducted from annual leave balance.

Employees can view their leave balance through the employee portal.
