College Library Policy

The college library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days and from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays.

Library Membership

All enrolled students receive library membership after registration. Students must carry their college identity card when borrowing books.

Book Borrowing

Each student can borrow up to 4 books at a time. Books are issued for a period of 14 days.

Renewal

A borrowed book can be renewed once for another 7 days if no other student has reserved the book.

Late Returns

A fine of 2 rupees per book is charged for each day after the due date.

Reference Books

Reference books are available for use inside the library only and cannot normally be taken home.

Digital Resources

Students can access subscribed digital journals and e-books through the college library portal using their student credentials.

Lost Books

If a student loses a borrowed book, the student must report it to the library immediately. The library may require the student to replace the book or pay the applicable replacement cost.

Library Rules

Students should maintain silence inside the reading area. Food and beverages are not permitted near books or library computers.

Library Assistance

Students who need help finding books or accessing digital resources can contact the library help desk during library working hours.
