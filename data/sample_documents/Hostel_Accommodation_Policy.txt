Hostel and Student Accommodation Policy

The college provides on-campus residential accommodation for full-time enrolled students across distinct residence halls.

Room Allocation and Eligibility
Hostel accommodation is allocated at the commencement of each academic year based on distance from home and academic standing. Rooms are available in double-sharing and triple-sharing configurations. Single occupancy rooms are reserved for final-year students and researchers based on merit. Students must submit an accommodation application accompanied by proof of admission and residential identity.

Hostel Fees and Deposit
The hostel fee is 35,000 rupees per semester, which includes room rent, water, and electricity charges. An additional refundable caution deposit of 5,000 rupees must be deposited upon initial admission. The caution deposit is refunded after deducting any dues or property damage upon final clearance when leaving the hostel.

Hostel Fee Refund Policy
If a student vacates the hostel or withdraws admission within 15 calendar days from the date of allotment, 80% of the semester hostel fee is refunded. If the withdrawal is submitted between 16 and 30 calendar days, 50% of the semester fee is refunded. No refund of the semester fee is granted for withdrawals after 30 days from allotment. The caution deposit remains 100% refundable subject to room clearance and submission of the no-dues certificate.

Mess Timings and Dining Regulations
The hostel mess serves nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals daily. The mess schedule is as follows:
1. Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM.
2. Lunch: 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM.
3. Evening Snacks: 4:30 PM to 5:30 PM.
4. Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM.
Taking mess utensils or food plates outside the dining hall into student rooms is strictly prohibited.

Gate Timings and Curfew Regulations
The main hostel entry gates are locked at 10:00 PM every night. All residents must mark biometric attendance between 9:00 PM and 9:45 PM. Students requiring late entry after 10:00 PM for academic projects or emergencies must obtain written permission from the Chief Warden at least 6 hours in advance. Overnight leave or night-out passes require an approved electronic request verified by the student's registered parent or local guardian.

Visitor Regulations
Visitors and day scholars are permitted only in the designated ground-floor visitor lounge between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM on weekdays and 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekends. Visitors are not allowed inside student rooms or residential corridors under any circumstances. Overnight stays by visitors or non-residents in student rooms are strictly prohibited.

Electrical Appliances and Maintenance
Heavy electrical appliances, including electric room heaters, induction stoves, and immersion rods, are prohibited in student rooms for safety reasons. Laptops, mobile chargers, study lamps, and personal hair dryers are permitted. Routine room maintenance and plumbing inspections are carried out on alternate Saturdays with prior notice.

Discipline and Conduct
The hostel maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging, harassment, alcohol, smoking, and substance abuse. A strict quiet study period is observed across all hostel wings between 10:30 PM and 6:00 AM. Violations of hostel regulations will result in disciplinary committee hearings, fines, or immediate cancellation of hostel allotment.
