Academic and Examination Policy

This document outlines the evaluation structure, examination regulations, grading norms, and attendance requirements for all undergraduate and postgraduate academic programs.

Attendance Requirement
Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in every enrolled theory and laboratory course during the semester. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% due to medical reasons or approved institutional representation may apply for attendance condonation by submitting medical certificates to the Dean of Academic Affairs. Students with attendance below 65% in a course are detained and barred from appearing in the end-semester examination for that course.

Evaluation Structure and Weightage
Course performance is evaluated through a combination of Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and the End-Semester Examination (ESE):
1. Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 40% weightage, comprising mid-term quizzes, assignments, and practical demonstrations.
2. End-Semester Examination (ESE): 60% weightage, covering comprehensive syllabus topics administered centrally by the Controller of Examinations.

Grading System and Calculation
The college follows a 10-point letter grading system:
- O (Outstanding): Grade Point 10.0 (90% to 100%)
- A+ (Excellent): Grade Point 9.0 (80% to 89%)
- A (Very Good): Grade Point 8.0 (70% to 79%)
- B+ (Good): Grade Point 7.0 (60% to 69%)
- B (Above Average): Grade Point 6.0 (55% to 59%)
- C (Pass): Grade Point 5.0 (50% to 54%)
- F (Fail / Arrear): Grade Point 0.0 (Below 50%)
The minimum passing mark for any course is 50% combined score (CIA + ESE). Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) are computed at the end of each semester.

Revaluation and Paper Review Procedure
A student who is dissatisfied with their awarded marks in the end-semester examination may apply for photocopy verification or paper revaluation:
1. Revaluation application must be submitted within 10 working days from the publication of official results through the student examination portal.
2. A prescribed revaluation fee of 500 rupees per course must be remitted.
3. The answer script is evaluated independently by an external senior examiner. If the revised score varies by 5% or more from the original score, the new score replaces the previous mark in the official grade card.

Supplementary and Makeup Examinations
Supplementary examinations for courses with grade F (backlogs) are conducted within 30 days after the declaration of end-semester examination results. Students who were absent during regular examinations due to certified hospitalization or bereavement may apply for a special makeup examination with the approval of the Academic Council without paying repeat course fees.

Academic Probation and Progression Criteria
Students must maintain a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 5.0 to remain in good academic standing. If a student's CGPA drops below 5.0 at the end of any academic year, they are placed on Academic Probation and must attend mandatory tutorial mentoring sessions. Students on academic probation cannot register for excess elective credits.

Hall Ticket and Examination Hall Protocols
All eligible candidates must download and print their official Examination Hall Ticket prior to the examination date. Candidates must carry both their physical hall ticket and valid college photo identity card into the exam hall. Entry is not permitted 15 minutes after the scheduled commencement of the examination. Programmable calculators, mobile phones, smartwatches, and notes are strictly forbidden inside examination halls.

Examination Malpractice and Disciplinary Actions
Any possession of unauthorized study materials, copying, or communication inside the examination hall is classified as academic malpractice. Cases are immediately referred to the Examination Malpractice Committee. Penalties range from cancellation of the examination paper to suspension from the college for one or more academic semesters.
